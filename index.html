Lion

The lion is a large wild cat known for its strength and social behavior. Lions live in groups called prides and are native to parts of Africa and India.

Red Panda

The red panda is a small mammal native to the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China. It has reddish-brown fur, a long bushy tail, and spends much of its time in trees.

Wolf

Wolves are social carnivorous mammals that belong to the dog family. They are known for living in packs, communicating through howls, and working together when hunting.
